# Phase40 performance admission

Decision: retain checked06 after complete semantic, execution and compiler-cost
review. Installation, all 42 CLI checks and the independent final audit pass.
The selected API is
`630879d8f030241a1d2c56e97f18f88b5be2070ac45dd02304afd06b3e3c5c0a`.
The incremental denominator is Phase39 checked05, not the Phase37 denominator
used in the previous campaign. TypeScript remains pinned to
`018751270e800bc222a93dad7f257083ee53a5f7`.

## Mechanism and scope

Keep the existing structural worker representation and complete typed graph
proof. Extend it to canonical Nat producers returning data, direct transfers
to proper descendants, and one-child constructor/known-combiner continuations.
Admit only the exact built-in `List<&2,U32>` representation. Keep intermediate
tagged lists and trees, original child aliases, ordered arguments and public
fallback. No fusion, new runtime helper, module, type or optimizer IR is added.

The actual-emission controls separately exercise complete values, deep 30,000
level inputs, mixed unary/binary frames, before/child/after argument ordering,
live dependency mutation and refusal shapes. Saved-JavaScript prototype results
cannot substitute for these checked compiler emissions.

## Rejection and correction

Checked05 passed the full 45-point output comparison, but original raytrace
rose from 777.016 ms to 1884.697 ms per timed call: a 2.426× slowdown, with all
three paired samples slower and disjoint observed ranges. Its newly selected
Nat-to-scalar component bypassed an existing faster scalar island. The complete
failed performance candidate and its 669 primary samples remain preserved.

Checked06 restricts new Nat-first admission to data results. The complete
raytrace and active-ray generated modules now exactly match Phase39. A separate
47.620-second screen confirms original raytrace at 0.9997× baseline/candidate;
it also retains tree and list gains and an unchanged scalar control. The screen
does not replace or pool with the final full-protocol ray measurements.

not 45 newly timed checked06 points. Identity reuse applies to generated-program

## Costs and adverse observations

The historical Mandelbrot point's changed module has a 9.684% slower median,
three paired losses and two wins, with overlapping and bimodal observed times.
Its two varied points are approximately unchanged. Retain this unresolved
adverse observation; do not average it away or claim every changed program wins.
RLE is about 0.8% slower with overlapping ranges; the expression points are
approximately unchanged despite different generated bytes.

Byte-identical controls also move: varied local folds appear about 7% slower,
while BST32 appears 4.3% faster with disjoint ranges. They demonstrate why
paired ranges alone do not establish compiler attribution. The full per-point
table, rather than an aggregate speed number, is the result.

All 36 normal checked-library requests reproduce their independently acquired
bytes. Request medians change by +18.44% local, +24.45% tree, −0.40% numeric and
+19.09% list. Tree/list are slower in all three pairs with disjoint ranges;
their added median costs are 484.83 ms and 317.18 ms respectively. Local ranges
overlap; numeric is approximately unchanged. Requests take 4.66–8.37× TypeScript
time on these four sources. The [compiler-cost report](compiler-cost.md) separates
host import, request and full process boundaries. These are real costs to retain
alongside the selected program-execution gains, not a compiler-throughput gain.

The tree/list execution gains justify this bounded tradeoff for the selected
release. Recovering analysis cost is a subsequent optimization target; no claim
is made that compiling and running each program once necessarily becomes faster.

Compiler source grows 141 physical lines (+0.753%) and 17 definitions to
18,863 lines / 2,104 definitions. The 70 modules, 71 types, 640 laws and runtime
bytes remain unchanged. This is an execution improvement with a small source
cost, not a reduction in line count. Separate final profiles and all inherited
plus new semantic closures pass. The preinstallation audit closes all required
groups against the exact selected source. The [installed release](release-06.md)
passes all 42 ordinary/relocated CLI checks and the 15-group final audit.

## Deferred work

The lexer complete-component prototype improves its three fixed inputs by
roughly 2×, but remains manual JavaScript. Production integration needs a wider
String/Char/Sigma proof boundary. It is deferred rather than included in the
release. Direct unfused lists provide a sound future denominator for testing
fusion; this campaign supplies no measured fusion benefit.

The next efficiency improvement should reduce repeated gate preparation and
brittle diagnostic assumptions using stable proof-owner identities. Preserve
semantic assertions, independent oracles and exact generated-byte provenance;
do not optimize the workflow by dropping inconvenient failures.
