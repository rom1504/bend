# Preserve the physical producer of a private, singly reloaded spill

The Phase198 assembly projection removed three producer spills from the repeated edit-distance row and measured 0.91036 and 0.91758 candidate/incumbent elapsed-time ratios across two assembly layouts. The separately tested addition of caller registers did not pass both layouts. This Phase199 candidate implements just the first mechanism in Bend, over the compiler's selected machine items. The qualifiers use ordinary loop-worker structure, exact producer identity and x86 operand semantics.

For each ordinary backedge worker of at most 256 IR statements and 640 selected items, the pass admits at most eight full-width MOVQ stores from RAX or R11 to an aligned private slot outside the save area. The actual frame map must own an eight-byte X181Stack at that displacement. The only subsequent use can be a full-width MOVQ reload within 24 intervening items in the same straight-line block. Every intervening instruction has two explicit, approved operands and neither names the producer register at any width or through an effective address. A bounded suffix must terminate by jumping directly to the current worker's own loop label. Any alternate entry into the store-to-reload region would require a label and therefore fails admission. The entire selected function must contain exactly two direct or overlapping observations of the slot. Indexed frame-pointer addressing refuses the optimization. The initial stack write and reload both disappear for an identical destination. Otherwise the load becomes a 64-bit register move. Flags and producer lifetime stay intact.

The measured compiler uses the usual ABI and fallback. Explicit branches bound the expensive lookahead and whole-function census. The first checked source acquisition rejected a missing affine sharing annotation. The corrected source preserves that failed receipt and adds explicit evaluation bounds.
