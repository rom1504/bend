# Accounting boundary

The declared observed windows total **132.27 minutes**: the initial root window
is 6.15 minutes and the resumed window 126.12 minutes through evidence-closure
preparation. About 29.89 hours lie outside those windows, including the interruption
and uncertain early agent continuation. Final archiving and publication after
the cutoff are excluded. Windows include reasoning, editing and waiting; they
are not continuous compute time or an estimate of model latency.

The ledger has absolute intervals for **36.42 minutes** of recorded jobs. This
is deliberately incomplete: several earlier comparison/acquisition events retain
their measured duration without an absolute enclosing interval, so the accounting
tool lists their evidence but does not invent start/end times. For example, the
rejected checked05 full comparison took 1,088.826 seconds and the lexer confirmation 227.097
seconds. Those durations are in their raw reports and cannot simply be added to
merged wall coverage without checking overlap. The [full accounting](report.md)
keeps these categories distinct.

The 90–110-minute forecast was missed. This campaign does not establish a causal
Sol/Astra or effort-level speed multiplier, dollar cost or general gains/hour
number. It does deliver the separately measured list/tree execution improvements,
with compiler-cost and source-growth tradeoffs. Exact-byte evidence reuse avoids
rerunning 42 unchanged comparisons; corrected three-ray execution took 79.463 seconds.
