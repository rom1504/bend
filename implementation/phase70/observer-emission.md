a borrowed traversal and an owned result adapter. Ordinary worker admission and
the old C body remain available. Raw compilation passes an explicit False
permission and never builds observer plans or workers. The checked certificate

scheduler entry hooks; borrowed bodies contain no allocation or ownership
mutation. Presence is distinguished from dynamic execution coverage.
