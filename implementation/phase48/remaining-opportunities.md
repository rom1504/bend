# Phase 48 remaining main-program opportunities

RNFA04's selected source mechanisms address a public composite boundary,
canonical native String dispatch, finite literal decoding and typed Array effects.
They do not establish general TypeScript parity. The main-program frontier is
still dominated by unsupported function flow, intermediate/persistent structures,
and permission costs relative to trivial work. Final corpus/release numbers
belong to the root campaign; the evidence below motivates bounded next work.

## First: matched recursive factories on a real main program

Morning is a concrete coverage gap. H02 passes 26 complete fixture oracles and 39
boundary observations, yet its acquired Morning and closure modules are entirely
byte-identical to array06 and contain zero H declarations. This is a mechanism
with no demonstrated representative gain, not an optimization of Morning.
Do not retain its 406-line implementation just because independent examples work.

The actual factory diagnostic finds 14 source definitions,28 lambda sites and
finite first-order captures. It also shows why a leading-lambda rewrite misses
Morning: Str.split produces a matcher-selected unary callback; Str.join.go
produces a matched two-argument head/separator stage and recursively binds its
head, leaving a separator callback. Factory prefix demand precedes the later
actual. A single saturated unary family pass would change coverage incorrectly.

The next experiment should implement one bounded matched-family mechanism,
with no source-name recognizer. Its admission should cap two factory components,
three stage signatures, two alternatives per stage, one known partial-binding
stage, three first-order capture fields/site, eight contextual callback helpers
and 64 graph definitions. Unbound and head-bound join stages need distinct closed
types or explicit checked stage proofs. Unknown flow, function-valued captures,
escape, mixed families and exhausted budgets refuse the complete root.

The cheapest falsifier is an independently renamed recursive source that
exercises both the matched factory and partial head binding. Require actual
family-entry counters, unequal capture values, noncommutative bodies, retained
callbacks, under/oversaturation and failure in the factory prefix before a later
actual. Add source/native mutation, errors and reentry with restored proof state.
Then acquire actual Morning and show changed reachable code plus executed family
entry before timing it. An unchanged module is a stop signal. Preserve original
argument/matcher demand and the existing native/machine stack strategy; a direct
native recursive rewrite is not justified by a known function target.

Evidence: [higher-order outcome](higher-order.md),
[bounded matched-family design](../../design/phase48/higher-order.md), and
[factory feasibility](evidence/function-factory-feasibility-v2.json). These facts
support implementability under the stated bounds, not complete admission or speed.

## Next: one dominant aggregate consumer, rather than a broad convention

The V scalar/vector experiments actually remove constructors, but their mixed
or weak measured screens do not earn the 477/451 code-line analysis cost. Both
remain deferred. Wider signatures, copied registers and continuation frames are
possible tradeoffs; no particular V8 cause was independently established.

Expression remains a useful narrower consumer: an already-private producer
materializes a complete Lit/Add/Mul/Sub tree, and the next private fold walks it.
A producer/algebra composition could avoid the tree and producer unwind work,
but must retain child order, U32 overflow, field demand and errors. First use a
saved-output causal ablation and complete independent oracle. Do not infer a
production opportunity solely from constructors appearing in source.

RLE is a different limit. Its state helper rebuilds nested transient tuples,
but its output list is consumed by both length and expansion. Removing that
shared persistent output by single-consumer fusion is not justified. A future
private product convention should target an observed consumer that removes
substantial work without widening every worker frame. Require dynamic transport
counts and clean speed evidence before another common pass is selected.

Evidence: [aggregate outcome and preservation](aggregate-transport.md) and
[exact output census](frontier.md). Fewer shells are a mechanism metric, not
zero allocation or a forecast of runtime improvement.

## Entry costs remain material, and correctness sets their floor

RNFA03's core8 screen leaves scalar-zero 63.137× TypeScript while the 8192-step
sibling is 1.138×. The separate allocation-free guard experiment improves the
short fold only 1.0392× and is neutral/adverse at larger sizes. Unsafe complete
admission bypass is only a diagnostic upper bound; it cannot justify caching
mutable host/dependency permission or omitting generic-path observations.

RNFA04's literal policy declines only proved direct countdowns zero/one. Its
supplementary screen recovers the large previous regressions but still shows
zero 1.75917µs versus baseline 1.34669µs, and one 5.92396 versus 4.99328µs. Larger
literal loops improve strongly but remain above TS on these inputs. This scoped
policy is not a general solution to public-entry cost, nor an empirical threshold
that should spread to unrelated loops. A next experiment needs an explicit
nonobservability or amortization contract, not a clean-host assumption.

Evidence: [entry guard ablation](entry-profitability.md),
[RNFA03 checkpoint](rnfa03-checkpoint.md), and the root-owned
`selfhost/build/phase48/literal-scale-screen-rnfa04/report.json`.
These screens are not additional weights in the maintained 45-point campaign.

## Other specific coverage boundaries

Finite literals do not enter the separate JW numerical path in Mandelbrot or
Symreg: their isolated F candidate emits baseline-identical modules. Carrying
an observable finite leaf into JW is future work requiring its own complete
host/demand proof. Broader constant folding cannot delete shared-view writes.

Unicode now removes proved String.append dispatch, but code-point traversal,
persistent split/join lists and continuation components remain. Map/Set native
admission also remains partial; diagnose actual refused helpers and observer
paths before broadening native operations. Evening's acyclic float-array block
is deliberately left generic after the first correct adapter regressed 34.91%.
Its main additionally includes Set, parsing and numeric showing; optimizing a
float-array diagnostic is not equivalent to accelerating the whole main.

Generic row is an actual successful composition: the RNFA03 core8 screen checks
all four returned arrays and gains 13.431×, yet still costs 4.074× TS. Original
handles, public shell, native read-result tuples and final serialization remain.
Any further raw-result transport must prove sharing and delayed field demand;
a partial checksum observation cannot replace the maintained full-output oracle.

Preserve refused/failed attempts, exact H/V payloads, and the 103 unrelated files.
Use one narrow actual-entry witness and a nominal core8 screen before another
full campaign. Writing this note performed no new implementation or target job.
