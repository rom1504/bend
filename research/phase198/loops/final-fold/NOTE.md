# Final fold gap: loop throughput at large n, extra fixed cost at small n

**New evidence:** all three current `orbit-native 45-01` fold objects are byte-identical to their Phase197 counterparts. The retained live-runtime results versus mature Bend C are **2.016× at 4096**, **2.021× at 8192**, and **2.681× at 128**. Their geometric mean is 2.219×: the family number hides a larger small-input penalty. Ordinary orbit linking gives 2.025×/2.026×/2.840× respectively. These are the four-round retained observations, not new runs; the ordinary large candidate also retains one slow outlier.

The actual direct hot loop still executes **18 instructions per cell**, including control, with **zero calls and zero frame operands**. All sizes have the same body. Mature C executes **15 instructions per two cells**, using memory-source ADD, one induction increment/compare per two cells, and an odd-element remainder. It still performs both dependent accumulator additions and both array stores. Its complete harness function also contains inline allocation and 32 vector stores initializing the 128-cell array; it has not removed the computation or memoized complete calls.

A descriptive fit through the 4096/8192 live medians gives **1.338ns per extra cell plus 108ns fixed cost**, versus **0.660ns plus 68ns** for C. The incremental-cost ratio is 2.027×. Ordinary orbit has the same 1.338ns slope and 133ns intercept. This is cross-point evidence, not a causal allocation/harness decomposition: seeds differ, and the C 128 point does not follow that fitted intercept. Nevertheless, a fixed allocation or harness cost cannot explain the roughly 2× additional cost of the extra 4096 cells.

Retained physical samples sharpen the reference side: in the **exact same mature-C binary** used now, **528/532 samples**,99.30% after period weighting, land inside the two-cell loop `[0xf 880,0xf 8b 2)`, rather than merely somewhere in the enclosing `p93.loop` harness function. Historical direct samples put 522/523 leaves in its fold worker, but that was a different Phase188 binary; no current direct percentage is inferred. The authenticated earlier counter run uses byte-identical direct objects and records one ARRAY_NEW per complete invocation, no per-cell external constructor/take/operation calls. It does not measure internal allocation, reset or reclamation time.

**Conclusion:** the large-input gap is most consistent with remaining loop instruction/dependency throughput. Small-input fixed overhead is material, but the available evidence cannot divide it among construction/reset, function boundaries, ownership and harness. No current physical profile or hardware-counter attribution was acquired.

**One source-level falsifier, not a new obligation:** manually unroll only `fold.loop` by two using two sequential original `fold.cell` calls, decrement fuel by two, and preserve exact zero/one fallbacks, index wrap, store order and the ordinary harness/allocation. Retain arbitrary seeds and capacity-boundary inputs. If ordinary emission produces a shorter two-cell recurrence and materially closes the 4096/8192 gap while allocation is identical, that supports a generic recurrence/unrolling pass. If it only duplicates instructions or spills, stop: the specific falsifier failed, and C's memory-operand/address selection remains separate. The existing uniform-fresh-array shortcut applies only through 128 cells and cannot explain or fix the two large points.
