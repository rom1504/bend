The next architecture step should preserve **typed owned extraction through the recursive region**, followed by **flat product results across that region**. Eliminating explicit frame storage ranks third: the selected transformation has already removed generic dispatch and register-bank handoffs, while non-tail recursion still has live captures that must be stored somewhere. This ranking concerns removable work and implementation boundaries; current evidence does not establish its dynamic cost distribution or predict a speedup.

The root's selected three-kernel screen improved the geometric mean ratio to upstream from 11.3635× to 4.6397×. Selected medians were tree 334 ms, list 290 ms and expression 134 ms. The separate packed-Take1 screen improved its table parent by about 1.6%; it used a different parent and remains deferred. These are three-program results, not full-corpus parity.

Concrete selected-code evidence supports the distinction. In `p114-first-unwind-screen01-coverage-expression-128-emit/program.ssa`, `eval` starts at line 8980 and retains one unary and three binary `p111_rt_take` sites. Its six resume cases save either the right subtree or the computed left result. Those values are required by the strict evaluation order; local labels alone cannot keep every outstanding recursive value in fixed locals. The list's `keep_gt1` and `dbl` workers (lines 8917 and 9194) each take a two-field cell; `dbl` reconstructs a cell after resuming. Typed extraction alone does **not** eliminate that output allocation or prove shell reuse.

Tree `scan` (line 13386 of its selected QBE) takes a unary leaf, issues two generic keeps on its scalar payload, and constructs a four-field result with `p111_rt_ctor(..., 4)`. The source declares `Stat = St{lo,hi,ok,mx: U32}` and joins two recursively returned Stats (`phase93/fixtures01/tree-bitonic.bend:20,112,118`). The selected H worker still returns one Term. Extending the already existing product/layout plan to recursive results and captures is therefore a concrete representation opportunity; replacing the entire Tree/List representation is not yet justified. It needs exact scalar/ownership facts, closed call/continuation shapes and boxing at escape boundaries, rather than a name-specific optimization.

There is also a smaller transport coverage gap: the selected expression worker still calls `p111_rt_frame` at three renamed `p114h_case_*_local_scratch` sites, and tree `scan` at one, although entry pushes and unwind pop/get are expanded. Extending the existing authenticated buffer coverage could remove those calls. It would retain frame storage and should be evaluated separately from the architectural choices above.

The extraction boundary is detailed in `next-owned-access.md`: `p111_take` discards the matched constructor name; the checked hit-arm owner consumption and field mask are still available immediately before that point. A known CID does not imply unique ownership, dynamic storage, sealed fields or an always-packed unary value. Preserve those separate obligations. Product results likewise do not justify changing source evaluation order, dropping aliases or bypassing unknown/escaping calls.
