# F32 backedge inspection and redirect

Numeric's actual final194 `direct.p37.numeric` loop carries one F32 word in RDX. Each positive iteration loads old x into XMM twice (0x408385,0x40839f), publishes its updated float through XMM→EDI then RDI→RDX (0x4083bd,0x4083c1), and reloads it for checksum arithmetic (0x4083e1). The backedge itself already coalesces this value. Thus the immediate carry witness is only four MOVD bank transfers and one GPR move, with no associated per-iteration spill. `numeric-loop-baseline.asm` and `numeric-loop-ir.json` preserve the evidence. Other MOVDs in the loop are constants or required conversion/sanitization operations and cannot all be assigned to carry overhead.

Persistent XMM carry would need an initial low32 load, a parallel XMM backedge update, stable register reservation against closed-region homes, and a certificate that ordinary/full64-bit observers either receive publication or refuse. It must retain old parameter values until their last read, including crossed/multiple backedges. The smallest guarded call-free form would miss ray's returning paths, which still call opcode25; specialized sqrt also retains a cold real libm call. All XMM registers are caller-clobbered by the existing ABI. Existing float analyses deliberately cut at these ordinary calls, so treating specialized sqrt as unconditionally pure would be unsound without cold-path live-value saves and preserved errno/MXCSR behavior.

No carry prototype was implemented: root redirected to the much larger concrete parallel-copy fallback on ray's second Again edge. That mechanism is independent of floating arithmetic and recorded in `parallel-cycle-v1.md`.
