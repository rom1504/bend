[review receipt](../../selfhost/tools/performance/phase42/review/equality-domains-v1-review.json)
and [source patch](../../selfhost/tools/performance/phase42/calls/equality-domains-v1/source.patch).

must not be rebound to checked16.

## Predicted inherited list-gate conflict disproved

Static review initially predicted that Phase40's ordinary composite list bench
would cease entering its producer/filter/map/fold workers once fusion activated.
Inspection of the actual checked16 fixture emission disproves that prediction.
Exactly two fusion markers occur in public ground_bench and chain_bench roots.
The composite bench contains no fusion marker; its two direct helper pipelines
still invoke all eight global structural stage workers. The existing ordinary
bench worker-entry assertion therefore remains meaningful and unchanged in the
final recipe.

that every caller takes that route. Activation must be read from actual checked
