## Runtime and accepted costs

The final preset600 protocol passes all 45 points and 669 fresh samples, using
three serial 15-point batches, unchanged warmup/calibration/role ordering and
checked module identities. The point-weighted geometric ratio to TypeScript
moves from 8.875959 for checked16 to 6.161075 for checked14: a 1.440651× aggregate
improvement over the preserved baseline. Equal-family and equal-source
improvements are 1.350004× and 1.355314×. Of 45 strict median comparisons, 25 improve
and 20 regress; the largest median regression is 14.5843%. Two candidate points
are faster than TypeScript, and two are within 10%. Overall TypeScript parity is
not achieved. These exposed 23-source measurements and paired ranges do not
establish universal performance or statistical significance.

Compiler-cost evidence is separate: the inherited four controls and four changed
families each have 36 rotated fresh requests across TypeScript, checked16 and
checked14, for 72 requests total. Root explicitly accepts Map request ratio4.1049,
lexer1.715 and BST1.3103, plus Map output-byte ratio2.216. Comparable compiler source
lines grow from20,056 to21,440, and API bytes from1,333,053 to1,460,868. Generated
program improvements do not imply compiler-throughput improvement. Profiles are
separate attribution evidence and are not substituted for final timing.
